# Privacy Policy — Cube Solver

**Last updated:** 2026-09-11  
**Developer:** Windsor Royal Apps (`windsorroyalapps`)  
**Contact:** Open an issue at https://github.com/windsorroyalapps/RubiksCubeSolver or contact the publisher via the Google Play store listing.

## Summary

Cube Solver is an **offline** Rubik’s Cube solver. The app does not require an account and, in the default build, does **not** collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

## Data we do not collect

By default the app does **not** collect:

- Name, email, or account credentials
- Precise or approximate location
- Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Crash or analytics telemetry (no third-party analytics SDK is bundled in this repository’s default configuration)

Solving happens entirely on your device using the native solver library.

## Purchases

Optional premium unlock (`premium_unlock`) is sold as a Google Play non-consumable in-app product. Payment details and purchase records are processed by **Google Play / Google LLC** under Google’s terms and privacy policy. The app stores a local entitlement flag (SharedPreferences) so premium features remain available offline after purchase or restore.

We do not receive your full payment card number.

## Permissions

The app requests **no dangerous/runtime permissions** beyond what the Play Billing library needs to talk to the Google Play Store when present. If Play Store / Billing is unavailable, purchase flows fail gracefully and free 3×3 solving still works.

## Children

The app is a puzzle utility suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised accordingly. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: contact Windsor Royal Apps via the Play listing or the GitHub repository issues page.
